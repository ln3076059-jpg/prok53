# Generalization boundary

All measured candidates are iterative development LOSO on gB-9, gZ-36 and gC-14. Their three outer folds contain no subject overlap with the corresponding inherited checkpoint or continuation training set. gZ-37 and gE-28 were not read for training, feature extraction, calibration, evaluation or model selection. Filesystem inventory alone does not consume a holdout.

No approved new independent subject pool is available. The repeated reuse of these three subjects precludes an independent 85% claim, even if a development score were to exceed it. Next work needs licensed new subjects, human-approved annotations, subject splits fixed before training, and one untouched evaluation after model freeze.
