# Literature Search Protocol
- **Date**: 2026-09-02
- **Databases**: IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, CVF Open Access, PubMed.
- **Queries**: 
  - `("Driver Monitoring System" OR "DMS") AND "Distraction" AND ("Deep Learning" OR "Computer Vision")`
  - `"Seatbelt Detection" AND "Occlusion"`
  - `"Multi-Object Tracking" AND "In-cabin"`
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Peer-reviewed journals/conferences (Q1/Q2/Top Tier), published 2017-2026, empirical results on DMS.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Non-vision sensors (Radar/EEG) unless fusion-focused, preprints without validation, basic tutorials.
